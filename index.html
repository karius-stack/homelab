Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. About this service

This is a privately operated Home Assistant instance used to control and monitor smart home devices and home automation systems.

2. Information we collect

Depending on usage, this service may process:

  • Name and email address provided by Google during sign-in.
  • Account identifiers and authentication information.
  • Home automation data, device states and sensor readings.
  • Technical information such as IP addresses and browser data.

3. Google OAuth

Google OAuth is used to authenticate users and control access to this Home Assistant instance. Google may provide basic account information such as name, email address and account identifier, depending on the permissions requested.

Google account information is used for authentication and access control, not for advertising or sale.

4. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, manage access, operate home automation features, troubleshoot the service and protect against unauthorized access.

5. Data storage

Home Assistant stores data on the systems used to operate the service, unless configured integrations transmit information to external services.

6. Data sharing

Personal information is not sold or shared for advertising. Data may be transmitted to third-party services when required by integrations explicitly configured in Home Assistant. Those services have their own privacy policies.

7. Data retention

Data is retained for as long as necessary to operate the service. The operator may delete stored data and associated account information.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the service. However, no internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.
